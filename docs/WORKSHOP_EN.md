# Advanced Security Systems (ADVSEC)

Arma 3 addon for servers with a **Military Police (MP)** and **Internal Affairs (IA)**. English and Spanish. ACE and CBA optional.

## Features
- **Identity documents**: passport, ID card, military ID, MP badge, IA credentials, permits and licences, shown as cards with a live face photo.
- **Access control**: keypads, door locks, alarms and CCTV with monitors, all linked by zone or network.
- **Police tools**: handcuff, escort, frisk, search vehicles (glovebox, trunk, hidden compartments), arrest and jail with persistent sentences, wanted list, fines, speed guns and fixed or portable speed cameras.
- **Forensics**: casings, fingerprints and blood, evidence bags with chain of custody, forensic lab, tampering and IA audits.
- **Internal Affairs**: suspicion index, automatic case files, suspensions, bank audits, live body cameras.
- **Reports**: MP and IA forms with status workflow, notes and access log.
- **AI road checkpoints**: officers stop every vehicle, check documents and the wanted list, fine and arrest.
- **Route patrols**: a commander draws a route on the map; the system logs whether the patrol completed it.
- **Military role-play**: ranks, 16 specialties with their own PDA, duty clock, 112 dispatch and panic button, base alert level, vehicle plates and dealer, K9.
- **Economy**: cash, bank, salaries, ATMs, arsenal-style shops and MP treasury. Compatible with the THUD HUD and Antistasi Ultimate.
- **Player database and superadmin terminal**: every player's record, editable in game.
- **TS_Lockers** personal lockers included (needs ACE and CBA).

Everything is configurable from Eden, Zeus, `description.ext` or CBA Addon Options. Language: CBA › General › Idioma / Language (automatic uses the game language; a dedicated server uses English unless you choose Español).
